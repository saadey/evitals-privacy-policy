eVitals Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

eVitals respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the eVitals mobile application.

eVitals is provided by EezyVitals. Website: eezyvitals.com

Information We Collect

eVitals may collect the following information to provide health tracking and wellness features:

  • Account information such as email address and profile information
  • Health information entered manually by users, such as blood pressure, heart rate, blood glucose, oxygen saturation, hydration, sleep, and other wellness records
  • Health tracking information and records created while using the app
  • Information required to generate health summaries and reports

How We Use Your Information

The information collected by eVitals is used to:

  • Provide health tracking and record management features
  • Display your health history and trends
  • Generate health reports and summaries
  • Improve application performance and reliability
  • Provide account-related services

Your Control Over Your Data

eVitals gives users control over their personal health information.

  • Users can delete individual health data entries added inside the app.
  • Users can request complete account deletion through Settings → Account → Delete Account.

Account Deletion

Users can permanently delete their eVitals account from within the application.

When an account deletion request is confirmed, associated account data and stored health information are removed according to our deletion process.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Sharing of Information

eVitals does not sell, rent, or trade your personal health information.

Your information is only used to provide the features and services available within the application, unless disclosure is required by law.

Third Party Services

eVitals may use trusted third-party services for essential application functionality, such as authentication, hosting, analytics, or crash reporting.

These services process information only as necessary to provide their intended functionality and are expected to maintain appropriate security practices.

Children's Privacy

eVitals is not intended for children under the age required by applicable laws without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
