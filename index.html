eVitals Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

eVitals respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is stored and retained, and the choices available to you when using the eVitals mobile application.

eVitals is provided by EezyVitals. Website: eezyvitals.com

Information We Collect

eVitals may collect information that you provide directly or that is generated through your use of the application, including:

  • Account information, such as your email address, name, and profile information.
  • Health information entered manually by you, such as blood pressure, heart rate, blood glucose, oxygen saturation, hydration, sleep, and other wellness records.
  • Health tracking information and records created while using the application.
  • Information required to generate health summaries, trends, and reports.
  • Technical information necessary to maintain application security, performance, and reliability.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected by eVitals to:

  • Provide health tracking and personal health record features.
  • Display your health history, records, and trends.
  • Generate health reports and summaries.
  • Provide account related services.
  • Maintain application security, stability, and performance.
  • Diagnose and resolve technical problems.

Health Information

eVitals allows users to record and manage personal health and wellness information. This information is used to provide the health tracking, history, reporting, and related features of the application.

eVitals does not sell or rent your personal health information to third parties.

Data Retention

eVitals retains your account information and health data for as long as your account remains active and as necessary to provide the application's features and services.

You may delete your account at any time through Settings → Account → Delete Account. When you confirm account deletion, your account information and associated health data are permanently deleted from our systems as part of our account deletion process.

Certain information may be retained for a limited period when required by applicable law, necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal requirements. Any such information will be securely deleted when it is no longer required for those purposes.

Your Control Over Your Data

eVitals provides users with control over their personal information and health records.

  • You can view and manage health information stored in your account.
  • You can delete individual health records where the application provides that functionality.
  • You can permanently delete your account through Settings → Account → Delete Account.

Account Deletion

Users can permanently delete their eVitals account from within the application.

Once account deletion is confirmed, the account and associated health information are deleted from our systems, subject to any limited retention required by applicable law or legitimate security and legal requirements described in the Data Retention section above.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Sharing of Information

eVitals does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or health information.

Information may be processed by service providers that support essential application functionality, such as authentication, secure data storage, analytics, or crash reporting. These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services.

We may also disclose information when required to do so by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

Third Party Services

eVitals may use trusted third party services to support application functionality, including authentication, hosting, analytics, crash reporting, and secure data storage.

These service providers may process limited information on our behalf and are expected to maintain appropriate security and privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

eVitals is not intended for children under the age required by applicable laws without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, our practices, or applicable legal requirements. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
